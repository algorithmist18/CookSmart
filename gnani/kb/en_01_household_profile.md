# Household profile

## Who eats

- About 4 people eat every day. If there are guests, each call says how many are eating today.
- People in the house: आरव (child), दादी (elder), पापा (adult), मम्मी (adult)

## Instructions for each person

- आरव: No peanuts at all (allergy), not even a little; Spice: mild; टिफ़िन में सब्ज़ी सूखी रखें; प्याज़ बारीक कटी हो, दिखे नहीं
- दादी: Soft, well-cooked food; mild spice; nothing very fried or heavy; रात का खाना हल्का रखें
- पापा: Less salt; no pickle, papad or extra salt on top; Much less sugar and sweets; less rice, potato and maida; more dal and sabzi
- मम्मी: Spice: medium

## Meal times

- नाश्ता: 8:00
- दोपहर: 1:00 (आरव का टिफ़िन 7:30 तक तैयार)
- रात: 8:30

## House rules and customs

- मंगलवार को प्याज़-लहसुन नहीं

## Important

These are instructions only. Never discuss anyone's illness or personal details. If she wants to know more, ask her to check with Madam/Sir.
