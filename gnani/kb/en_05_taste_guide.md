# Taste guide

## By the condition of the item

- **Tomato**: Very ripe tomatoes are sweet and juicy, best in dal, gravy and chutney; use firm ones for salad
- **Onion**: Well-cooked onion adds sweetness to gravy
- **Potato**: Older potatoes suit paratha and stuffing; new potatoes suit jeera aloo
- **Spinach**: Fresh spinach is greenest and sweetest; if wilted, blanch and do not overcook
- **Paneer**: Older paneer is better in bhurji or gravy than fried; add paneer last so it stays soft
- **Cauliflower**: Fresh gobhi is crisp when roasted; slightly older is good in gravy or paratha stuffing
- **Peas**: Fresh peas are sweet; do not overcook
- **Curd**: Sour curd is best for kadhi; fresh curd for raita and curd rice
- **Cream**: Add at the end, do not boil
- **Cucumber**: Firm cucumber in salad; soft in raita
- **Chicken**: Fresh chicken cooks quickly; do not overcook
- **Bhindi**: Wash, dry completely and then cut: less sticky
- **Brinjal**: Roast for bharta; fresh brinjal has fewer seeds
- **Cabbage**: Cook lightly to keep some crunch
- **Carrot**: Sweet; goes well with peas
- **Beans**: Do not overcook
- **Capsicum**: Add last to keep it crisp
- **Lauki**: A light sabzi; good with tomato
- **Mushroom**: Fry fast on high heat
- **Bread**: Day-old bread is crisp when toasted
- **Banana**: Ripe bananas are sweet and best in sweet dishes
- **Apple**: Firm apple in chaat

## For some dishes

- Palak Paneer: Blanch and grind the spinach to keep it green; add less cream, at the end; cook the paneer only the last 3-4 minutes
- Tomato Dal: Let the tomatoes cook down fully; ripe tomatoes are best here
- Dal Tadka: Add the tadka last and cover immediately
- Palak Dal: Add the spinach last so it stays green
- Matar Paneer: Do not overcook the peas; paneer last
- Aloo Gobi: Do not stir too much or the gobhi and aloo will break
- Bhindi Masala: Dry the bhindi completely before cutting and do not cook it covered
- Baingan Bharta: Roast the brinjal directly on the flame for a smoky taste
- Kadhi: Sour curd is best for kadhi; keep stirring until it boils
- Kheera Raita: Squeeze the water out of soft cucumber
- Poha: Do not soak the poha too long
- Aloo Paratha: Cool the filling before stuffing; older potatoes are good here
- Moong Khichdi: Light and easy to digest, good for anyone who is unwell or elderly
- Rajma Masala: Boil overnight-soaked rajma well; it must not be firm inside
- Chole: Boil overnight-soaked chana well; it must not be firm inside

## Note

These are tips, not rules. Her experience and the household's taste come first.
