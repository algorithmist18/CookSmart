# Units

## The household katori

- 1 katori = about 150 ml (this household's katori)
- 1 glass = about 200 ml
- Teaspoon = 5 ml; tablespoon = 15 ml

## Rough guide

- Roti: usually 2-3 per adult, 1-2 per child
- If unsure, use the quantities given on the call

## When she gives a quantity

If she says it in katori, convert to ml using the measure above, repeat it back and ask 'Is that right?'
