# Health notes

## What this is

General nutrition information, not medical advice. Whatever a doctor has told someone comes first.

## A balanced thali

- Dal or protein + sabzi + roti/rice + curd or salad
- Do not serve only one type of food (for example only rice and potato)
- Measure oil, ghee, salt and sugar

## Instructions for the family

- आरव: No peanuts at all (allergy), not even a little; Spice: mild; टिफ़िन में सब्ज़ी सूखी रखें; प्याज़ बारीक कटी हो, दिखे नहीं
- दादी: Soft, well-cooked food; mild spice; nothing very fried or heavy; रात का खाना हल्का रखें
- पापा: Less salt; no pickle, papad or extra salt on top; Much less sugar and sweets; less rice, potato and maida; more dal and sabzi

## What each item does for health

- **Tomato**: Vitamin C and lycopene
- **Onion**: Adds flavour; some fibre
- **Potato**: Energy and potassium; better boiled or roasted than fried
- **Spinach**: Iron, folate and vitamin K
- **Paneer**: Protein and calcium
- **Cauliflower**: Fibre and vitamin C
- **Peas**: Protein and fibre
- **Dal**: Protein and fibre
- **Rice**: Gives energy; serve with dal and sabzi
- **Atta**: Fibre; coarsely ground atta is better
- **Curd**: Good for digestion; calcium
- **Cream**: Rich, so keep the quantity small
- **Rajma**: Protein and fibre; soak overnight and boil well
- **Cucumber**: Lots of water, cooling
- **Milk**: Calcium
- **Egg**: Protein; always fully cooked
- **Chicken**: Protein; lighter without the skin
- **Bhindi**: Fibre
- **Brinjal**: Fibre
- **Cabbage**: Fibre and vitamin C
- **Carrot**: Vitamin A (beta-carotene)
- **Beans**: Fibre
- **Capsicum**: Vitamin C
- **Lauki**: Light and easy to digest; lots of water
- **Mushroom**: Protein and vitamin B
- **Moong**: Easy-to-digest dal
- **Masoor**: Protein and iron
- **Chana**: Protein and fibre; soak and boil well
- **Besan**: Protein; heavy when fried
- **Poha**: Light breakfast; better with vegetables
- **Suji**: Light; add vegetables
- **Bread**: Whole wheat is better than maida
- **Sabudana**: Gives energy, low in protein: serve with peanuts or curd
- **Kuttu**: Allowed in vrat; light to digest
- **Samak**: Allowed in vrat; light
- **Peanuts**: Protein and good fat, but never if there is a peanut allergy
- **Banana**: Energy and potassium
- **Apple**: Fibre

## Children and elders

- For children: mild spice, small pieces, well cooked
- For elders: soft, light and fresh food
